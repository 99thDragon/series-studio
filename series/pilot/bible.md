# Pilot: "Last Delivery"

Rin is a bike courier in a city where it never stops raining. Every episode is one delivery, and every package carries a story.

**EP01:** At midnight Rin picks up a glowing package. When she reads the label, every light in the city goes out, and she has to race through the dark to deliver the one light left.

## Rules every episode inherits
- Rin always looks like her reference frame: silver bob, amber eyes, yellow courier jacket, red sneakers.
- It is always night and always raining.
- No real brands, no real people.
