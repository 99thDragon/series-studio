# Tidewatch — Series Bible

## World
A storm-bound northern coast. The town of Gullwick lives under the Tidewatch lighthouse. When the lamp dims, the sea "remembers" — drowned things walk back to shore for one night.

## Tone
Quiet, melancholy, warm in small moments. Wonder over horror. Long silences are allowed.

## Story rules
- The lamp never goes fully dark on screen until the season finale.
- Magic is never explained in dialogue; it is shown through the sea.
- Mira is always the point of view. We never see a scene she could not have witnessed.

## Never
- No modern technology (no phones, cars, electric lights).
- No gore.
