# S01E01 — The Lamp Dims

## SC01 — Lamp room, dusk
Bram hands Mira the lantern and the keys. He leaves for the mainland.

BRAM: Keep it burning. Whatever you hear.

## SC02 — Lamp room, night
Mira dozes. The lamp gutters. Far below, a figure rises from the surf.

## SC03 — Harbor, night
Mira runs down to the harbor with her lantern. The flame turns blue. The drowned fisherman is waiting on the cobbles.
